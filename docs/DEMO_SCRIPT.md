# 3-minute demo script

1. Start on the incident queue.
2. Pick “Warehouse smoke near residential block”.
3. Say: “Instead of asking a generic chatbot what to do, we turn the report into an operational decision object.”
4. Click Analyze.
5. Point to the priority score and confidence.
6. Explain that the factors are visible, so a reviewer can challenge the decision.
7. Show detected risks: hazardous atmosphere, public exposure and secondary access risk.
8. Show recommended actions and the suggested FIRE / HAZMAT resource.
9. Point out the information gaps: affected people, response teams, evacuation status and last verified update.
10. Finish on “Decision audit”: every analysis creates a hash-linked event.
11. Closing line: “The AI proposes; the human remains accountable.”
